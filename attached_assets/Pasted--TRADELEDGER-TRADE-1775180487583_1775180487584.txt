================================================================================
TRADELEDGER — TRADE TYPE CUSTOMISATION (ADD-ON TO EXISTING BUILD)
================================================================================

This prompt modifies the existing TradeLedger app. Make the smallest possible
changes to achieve the goal. Reuse all existing screens, logic, and components
wherever possible. Do not rewrite anything that does not need to change.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GOAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Add a trade_type field to the user profile. During onboarding Step 1 (before
the existing Business Details fields), ask the tradie to select their trade.
Use this selection to pre-populate two things throughout the app:
  1. The default expense categories shown in Expense Capture
  2. The suggested quote line item templates in the New Quote form

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 — DATABASE CHANGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Add one column to the existing users table:
  trade_type VARCHAR(50) DEFAULT 'other'

Valid values:
  electrician | plumber | carpenter | builder | painter | hvac |
  tiler | landscaper | concreter | other

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 — ONBOARDING CHANGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

In the existing onboarding Step 1 screen, add a trade selector ABOVE the
Business Name field. Do not change anything else in the onboarding flow.

Show a scrollable grid of 10 tappable cards (2 columns), each with an emoji
and label. Selected state: border 2px solid #1A1A1A. Unselected: #F7F4F1.

Cards:
  ⚡ Electrician
  🔧 Plumber
  🪚 Carpenter
  🏗️ Builder / Renovator
  🖌️ Painter
  ❄️ Air Con / HVAC
  🟫 Tiler
  🌿 Landscaper
  🧱 Concreter
  📦 Other

Selection is required before "Continue" is enabled.
